Walking Island — Random item odds

As of November 17, 2026 · app 1.7.0 · Walking Island support

In Walking Island's Wave Shop you can buy an Egg or a Golden Egg with pebbles. Pebbles can be earned by walking and can also be bought with in-app purchases, so eggs count as randomized items. Which friend is inside is decided the moment you buy the egg, and it hatches once you have walked enough. In the app, tap the ? next to “Egg” in the Wave Shop to see the odds for your own island.

Egg

  • Contains one of the regular friends you haven't met yet. Every candidate is equally likely.
  • Chance per friend = 100% ÷ (number of regular friends you haven't met)
  • There are 23 regular friends. Once you've met all 23, an egg holds one of the 23, each 4.35%.
  • Legendary friends and the Christmas-only friend (Santa) never hatch from eggs.

Golden Egg

  • Contains one of the legendary friends you haven't met yet, each equally likely.
  • There is currently one legendary friend, so a Golden Egg holds that friend 100% of the time.
  • Once you've met every legendary friend, the Golden Egg is no longer sold.

Other

  • Treasure chests in the Playground are earned only by playing and can't be bought with money or pebbles. Their pebble odds are shown under ? in the Playground.
  • If the odds change, we update this page and the in-app notice together.
